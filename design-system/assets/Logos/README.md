# Logos

`racktrack-mark.jpg` is the italic R mark, white on black, 377 × 377 px. It comes from the current site (`assets/logo.jpg`) and is kept unchanged.

- In the nav, use it as a square tile at 26px with a 5px radius, followed by "RackTrack" in Schibsted Grotesk, weight 700, 17px.
- Keep a clear space of at least half the tile width around it.
- Don't recolour it (never green or amber), don't put it on a busy image, and don't stretch it.
