The story behind RackTrack

i · The moment

Three sources. Three different stories

It started in a rack audit where the spreadsheet, the switch labels and the live ports each told a different story, and a team that should have been planning a change was crouched in front of cabinets instead.

ii · The reason

One missed cable

One missed cable could delay a migration, or send someone back into the data hall after hours. The physical layer deserved the same confidence teams already expect from their cloud dashboards.
