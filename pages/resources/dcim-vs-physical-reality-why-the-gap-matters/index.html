Your DCIM platform shows a plan. The rack shows what's real

DCIM tools model what should be there. Knowing where that model and the real rack disagree helps you fix the right problem.

On this page

  1. What DCIM actually models
  2. Why the model and the room disagree

A DCIM platform is a floor plan of your infrastructure. A floor plan is not the building. Both are useful, but only one of them is true right now.

What DCIM is actually modeling

03 / 04 the cost

What it costs to trust the model blindly

Capacity planning built on the plan instead of the room tends to end one of two ways: buying more racks than necessary, or running out of power and space at the exact moment there's no time left to fix it.

DCIM is the map. The rack is the territory. When they disagree, the territory always wins. The only question is whether you find that out on a calm Tuesday or during an outage.

04 / 04 the fix

Using the model and the room together

The fix isn't replacing DCIM. It's treating a physical sweep as the regular reconciliation step that keeps the map honest, instead of assuming it's accurate simply because nobody's said otherwise.
